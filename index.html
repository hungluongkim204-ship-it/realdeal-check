6 pricing tricks to watch for

If you spot one of these, check the price history before you buy.

01

The inflated “was” price

The item sat at a high price for a few weeks just so the sale could compare against it. Compare against the 90-day low, never the crossed-out price.

02

“List price” nobody pays

RRP or MSRP is often a price no store actually charged. A discount off a fictional price is not a discount.

03

The special model number

Black Friday TVs and laptops are often unique SKUs with cheaper panels or less storage, which makes direct price comparison impossible.

04

Fake urgency

Countdown timers and “only 3 left” banners exist to stop you checking. Which? has repeatedly found the same price returns after the event.

05

“Up to 70% off”

The headline number applies to a handful of items. The product you want is probably 10–20% off.

06

Bundle padding

A “free” accessory or gift card in the bundle hides that the main item isn’t cheaper. Price the item alone.

FAQ

When is Black Friday 2026?

Black Friday is Friday, November 27, 2026. Thanksgiving is Thursday, November 26 and Cyber Monday is November 30.

How do I know if a Black Friday deal is real?

Compare the deal price with the lowest price the item sold for in the past 90 days, not with the crossed-out “was” price. If the deal is at least 5% below that 90-day low, it’s a real deal. If it’s about the same, it’s a normal price dressed up as a sale.

Are Black Friday deals cheaper than Cyber Monday?

It depends on the category. Adobe’s 2026 forecast expects the deepest electronics and computer discounts on Cyber Monday, and the best TV, toy, apparel, appliance and furniture deals on Black Friday.

Should I wait until after Black Friday?

If the price isn’t at its 90-day low, often yes. Consumer group Which? found that most tracked Black Friday deals were matched or beaten at some point in the months after the event.
