What we collect

RealDeal Check does not ask for an account, name or email. Prices you type into the deal checker are processed in your browser and are not sent to us.

Analytics

Our host may record anonymous, aggregated visit statistics (such as page views and country). These do not identify you.

Some outbound links, including links to Amazon, are affiliate links. When you click one, the destination retailer may set cookies to attribute a purchase to us. This does not change the price you pay. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases.

Links to price trackers and retailers lead to sites with their own privacy policies.
