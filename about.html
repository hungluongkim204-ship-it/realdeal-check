About the checker

A clearer answer to “is this actually a deal?”

RealDeal Check is a free tool that tells you whether a sale price is a genuine discount — in about ten seconds, without an account.

How the verdict works

You enter up to three numbers: the crossed-out “was” price, the current sale price, and optionally the lowest price the item sold for in the past 90 days (from a price-history tool like CamelCamelCamel, Keepa, or PriceSpy). The verdict is computed from exactly those numbers — nothing else:

  • Deal price vs. the 90-day low: if the sale price is meaningfully below the lowest price of the last three months, it’s a real deal. If it’s at or above that low, the “discount” is a normal price dressed up as a sale.
  • Claimed discount plausibility: how the advertised percentage-off compares with typical discount ranges for the category.
  • Category timing: based on Adobe’s 2026 US holiday forecast, the tool shows which sale day your category is expected to bottom out on — and whether waiting or buying now makes sense.

All statistics and forecasts shown on this site come from published sources — BCG’s 2026 study, Omni Calculator’s 2026 survey, Which?’s Black Friday investigations, and Adobe’s Digital Insights 2026 Holiday Forecast — cited where they appear.
